Before Activity 2 · 2–3 minutes (within the activity time)

From unconditional to conditional hypotheses

An unconditional hypothesis proposes a relationship between two variables. A conditional hypothesis proposes that this relationship depends on a third variable.

Unconditional hypothesisConditional hypothesis
Education increases electoral participation.Education increases electoral participation more strongly among younger citizens than among older citizens.
Economic insecurity increases support for far-right parties.Economic insecurity increases support for far-right parties more strongly among citizens with negative attitudes towards immigration.
Corruption reduces trust in government.Corruption reduces trust in government more strongly among citizens who do not support the governing party.
Schooling is positively associated with hourly earnings.The positive association between schooling and hourly earnings is stronger among women than among men.

These are illustrative hypotheses to be tested, not established empirical findings.

Key idea: The relationship between X (explanatory variable) and Y (outcome) depends on Z (moderating variable). An additive model assumes the same slope; an interaction model allows the slope to vary with Z.

Discuss with a partner

Identify X, Y and Z in each conditional hypothesis. In our workers example, X is years of schooling, Y is hourly earnings (£ per hour), and Z is gender. What would different slopes for men and women tell us?

Activity 2 · 5 minutes

What does an interaction do?

Y = β₀ + β₁X + β₂Z + β₃XZ. Here Y is predicted hourly earnings (£ per hour), X is schooling (years), and Z is female (0 = men, 1 = women).

Move the slider to change the interaction coefficient (β₃), not gender or schooling. β₃ is the difference between women’s and men’s schooling slopes. The slope for men stays fixed at 1.10; the slope for women changes. Start at zero and observe the parallel lines, then try positive and negative values. These hypothetical coefficients are not estimated from the dataset.

Activity 3 · 15 minutes

Schooling × gender

Your task: Estimate Model 5, then identify the reference group and calculate both schooling slopes before revealing the answer.

Hypothesis: schooling is more positively associated with earnings among women. Estimate the full interaction model.

regress earnings c.schooling##ib0.female experience urban ///
    parent_edu public_sector training
estimates store m5
estimates table m4 m5, b(%9.3f) stats(N r2)

Coefficient plot

coefplot m5, drop(_cons) xline(0) levels(95)

Points are coefficients; bars are 95% confidence intervals. Coefficients have different units. This plot is not a plot of conditional slopes. If needed, install coefplot once using ssc install coefplot.

## includes both constitutive terms and their product. The schooling coefficient is the slope for men; the female coefficient is the gender difference at zero schooling; the interaction is the difference in slopes.
